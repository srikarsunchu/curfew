Curfew
GitHubConnect your Whop

A fraud layer for Whop that acts while you sleep.

Curfew learns what normal looks like for your shop, watches every payment as it lands, and contains a card-testing attack before it turns into a hundred chargebacks. It refunds and revokes the batch in ten minutes, not when you wake up.

Connect your WhopTwo minutes. One API key. Free.
1. It learns your normal

Sixty days of your payments: rate per hour, first-time-buyer share, decline rate, average order, the countries you actually sell to.

2. It scores every payment

Six signals over a ten-minute window, each explained in plain numbers. A real launch fires two. Card testing fires five.

3. It acts, then asks

Three or more signals: alert, hold, then refund and revoke unless you press undo. Launch mode for drop days.

What it cannot do

  • It cannot decline a charge. Whop is the merchant of record. Curfew shrinks the damage window; it does not close it.
  • Refunds still cost the processing fee. You trade a small certain loss for avoiding disputes, which is what actually gets accounts suspended.
  • Your API key and webhook secret are encrypted at rest. Curfew can read your payments, refund them, and cancel memberships. Grant only the scopes listed on the connect page. Disconnect deletes everything.
